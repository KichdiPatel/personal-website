# PaperMarket

I kept wanting to test Polymarket trading ideas without risking real money every time I changed a bot. A backtest is useful, but I also wanted to see what would happen when a strategy actually placed orders against live markets. So I built PaperMarket.

The idea was pretty simple. Give yourself a paper trading account, point a Polymarket client at a different API, and try out your strategy before trading for real. I also made a dashboard for people who just wanted to place paper trades through a UI.

I built the API with FastAPI and Postgres. It handles accounts, API keys, orders, and trades, with endpoints shaped around Polymarket's CLOB API. I also built a separate fill engine that watches the order book for markets with open paper orders. When the best bid or ask crosses your price, it records a simulated trade.

One thing I liked about this project was figuring out how close I could get to the workflow of a real trading API while keeping the simulated side understandable. It is still a prototype, and the fills are much simpler than actual trading. They do not account for things like your place in the order queue or slippage. But it was a fun way to build and test trading tools without every bug costing money!
